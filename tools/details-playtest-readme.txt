LAST LIGHT — PROLOGUE NEIGHBORHOOD DETAILS (offline playtest)
===============================================================

These are the Prologue's remaining neighborhood details, rebuilt as authored models in the running game:
- the street signs;
- lawn and porch chairs and grills;
- garden hoses and sprinklers;
- the children's toys;
- kiddie pools, trampolines and swing sets;
- sheds and clotheslines;
- Briarwood's creek-side chain link and the lookout's field fence.

They await review: nothing here is approved yet.

No installation and no internet connection are needed. Everything is inside Last-Light-Playtest.html: the game, plus the
authored bicycles, houses, vegetation, pavement, street props and these details.

1. Unzip the folder anywhere.
2. Open Last-Light-Playtest.html in desktop Chrome or Edge (WebGL 2; only Chromium was tested).
3. Add one of these to the end of the address in the address bar and press Enter:

     ?details                                                  the neighborhood details alone
     ?bikes&houses&vegetation&pavement&streetprops&details     with all six visual benchmarks (recommended)

   Without ?details the Prologue is exactly the game as it was.

4. Press any key on the title screen, choose New Game, and ride (W to pedal, mouse to look, Space for the bell).

   Every sign, chair, toy, hose, swing set, trampoline, shed, clothesline and fence stands where it always stood, in its
   own yard and its own colours. The riding, the collisions and the story are unchanged. The old oak, its tire swing, the
   lookout bench, the chalk, the bikes and the friends' homes are not touched.

The keys added by the benchmark (they do nothing in the normal game):
   .  (Period)  AUTHORED <-> ORIGINAL details, in the same frame (an honest before/after)
   ,  (Comma)   LOD: automatic -> 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> automatic (to see what distant objects become)

The other benchmarks' keys still work: X / Z (street props), B (bikes), H (houses), K / Y (vegetation, lawns), O (pavement),
and so on.

The small DEV labels at the top-left say what loaded and how much it costs. If something fails to load, its label says so
in red, and the original objects simply stay.

The authored details are shown in the Prologue only. From the night chapters on (and in the credits and the 2026 coda) the
original objects show.

What to look at (review-gallery/index.html in this folder has the same views as before/after pairs, with a slider):
   - the street signs:
       - the street-name blades and the STOP sign at Briarwood Lane;
       - SPEED LIMIT 25, SLOW CHILDREN AT PLAY and NO OUTLET;
       - whether the lettering reads and the posts stand right.
   - the front yards: lawn chairs, the white porch chairs, toys (big wheels, wagons, scooters, small kid's bikes, balls),
     kiddie pools.
   - the garden hoses: each runs from a hose bib on the house's wall to its coil. Also the impact sprinklers turning,
     with their water.
   - the back yards, seen between the houses: trampolines (with their net), swing sets, sheds, clotheslines, kettle grills.
   - Briarwood's chain link along the creek, and the field fence behind the lookout's bench.
