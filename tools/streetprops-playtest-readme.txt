LAST LIGHT — PROLOGUE VEHICLES & STREET PROPS (offline playtest)
=================================================================

The Prologue's parked cars, the parent's minivan that comes home during the ride, the utility poles and their wires,
the streetlights, mailboxes, fire hydrants, trash carts, basketball hoops and Oak Hollow Drive's fences, rebuilt as
authored models in the running game. Awaiting review: nothing here is approved yet.

No installation and no internet connection are needed. Everything (the game, the authored bicycles, houses, vegetation,
pavement textures, and the street-props models and textures) is inside Last-Light-Playtest.html.

1. Unzip the folder anywhere.
2. Open Last-Light-Playtest.html in desktop Chrome or Edge (WebGL 2; only Chromium was tested).
3. Add one of these to the end of the address in the address bar and press Enter:

     ?streetprops                                        the vehicles and street props alone
     ?bikes&houses&vegetation&pavement&streetprops       with all five visual benchmarks (recommended)

   Without ?streetprops the Prologue is exactly the approved game.

4. Press any key on the title screen, choose New Game, and ride (W to pedal, mouse to look, Space for the bell). Every
   car, pole, lamp, mailbox, hydrant, bin, hoop and fence stands exactly where it always stood; the riding, the
   collisions and the story are unchanged. Around the middle of the street a minivan comes home down Briarwood and
   pulls into its garage, as before: it is the authored minivan now.

The keys added by the benchmark (they do nothing in the normal game):
   X   AUTHORED <-> ORIGINAL vehicles and street props, in the same frame (an honest before/after)
   Z   LOD: automatic -> 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> automatic (to see what distant objects become)

The other benchmarks' keys still work: B (bikes), H (houses), K / Y (vegetation, lawns), O (pavement), etc.

The small DEV label at the top-left says what loaded and how much it costs. If something failed to load it says so in
red, and the original objects simply stay.

The authored set is shown in the Prologue only. From the night chapters on (and in the credits and the 2026 coda) the
original objects show, so the later chapters (the police, the works, Chapter Eight's damage) are exactly as before.

What to look at (review-gallery/index.html in this folder has the same views as before/after pairs, with a slider):
   - the cars in the driveways: proportions, glass with the cabin behind it, paint, wheels, how they sit on the drive
   - a pole with a transformer: crossarms, insulators, cutouts, the can, the secondary rack, the wires between poles
   - the cobra-head streetlights, by day and lit at dusk near the end of the street
   - mailboxes (four kinds), the yellow hydrants, the carts at the curb, the hoops, the fences and side-yard gates
