The project’s earlier Jamie, Sam and Alex studies supplied the connected anatomical mesh, relaxed stance, wardrobe and footwear construction, facial topology and studio lighting. This adaptation changes the proportions and facial surface, eyes and complexion, hair and wardrobe. It adds shaped nail plates, hand pigment, static contact poses, a fitted first-person body, review props and camera studies. The companion hand in the contact study uses a local copy of Sam’s hand surface and skin material. The original cast models remain separately recoverable in the parent commit.
