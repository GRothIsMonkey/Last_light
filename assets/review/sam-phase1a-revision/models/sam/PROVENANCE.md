## Component history

| Component | Origin and treatment |
|---|---|
| Body, ears, fingers, feet, lips and eyelids | Derived from Child. Source seams were welded and compatible triangles rejoined. The source UV layout was retained. The face, shoulder width, limbs and stance were reshaped. A reversible wardrobe mask covers hidden anatomy. |
| Eyes, brows and expression | The source socket boundaries guided separate ocular surfaces. Dark iris and scleral colour, optical surface, lid margins, brows and lashes were authored for Sam. The edited body has one preview smile. |
| Hair | New scalp-following underlayer, independently editable directional locks and fine curves. The espresso-coloured fibre and lock maps belong to this review scene. |
| Wardrobe and footwear | Project garment and shoe construction methods were adapted for Sam. New henley facing, cream bindings, buttons, cargo pockets, trail-runner stays and toe guards establish his outfit. |
| Skin and surface maps | Eight authored image maps cover portrait colour, portrait roughness, limbs, iris, ocular surface, cotton weave, hair fibres and hair-lock colour/opacity. The supplied GLB contained no image maps. |
| Renders | Blender Cycles output. The two matched comparisons also depict Roth Studios’ approved Jamie checkpoint, which uses the same credited Child source. The comparison script reads Jamie’s existing scene and writes PNGs. |

## Targeted Phase 1A revision

The revised source scene changes Sam’s resting shoulders, arms and fingers, his facial anatomy and eyelid assembly, and the volumes and ends of his existing dark shag. These are further modifications to the credited anatomical source. The new matched renders use both revised scenes. The original GLB is shared at the top level of this package.
