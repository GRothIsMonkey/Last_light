### Changes for this review

The resting pose in each editable body now has lower shoulders, a small forward shoulder roll, different elbow bends on the two sides, and a gradual turn through the forearms. The four fingers bend by different amounts across their joints. The thumbs turn toward the fingers. The shirts follow the revised shoulders and cuffs. Sam’s lower arms have room for the raised cargo pockets.

Sam has a fuller outer cheekbone over a leaner lower cheek, a firmer jaw corner and chin, a softer transition from the nasal bridge into a broader rounded tip, and a wider mouth with a fuller lower lip. The upper lids, brow arch and lid margins were reshaped together. His complexion, teal henley, blue-gray shorts and smaller frame carry through from the prior scene.

The existing dark shag gained about eight millimetres at the top and a few millimetres at the temples. The ends of the layered locks vary in length and direction, with the fine hairs following their locks. The haircut still falls across his forehead.

### Editable sources and recovery

Open [Sam](models/sam/chr.sam.a12.blend) or [Jamie](models/jamie/chr.jamie.a12.blend) in Blender 5.2.2 LTS. Both scenes contain their texture images, editable mesh and curve objects, the original eight cameras, lighting and preview expression. The separate texture PNGs sit beside the scenes. To make the individual views again, open the embedded text “Re-render the eight review cameras.py” in Blender’s Text Editor and run it. Use [the comparison script](comparison/recreate-comparison.py) with the two revised scenes to recreate the shared views.

This revision starts from the Sam scene at `592af1a2440a1a7d83de1cc3a8a3294960f2f713` and Jamie at `f9060d00faec8d3cd2f2d9abd5dfa77d91e8cfe5`. The recovery package includes the local source GLB, scene files, textures, PNGs and a SHA-256 manifest. The visual decision at this checkpoint is the relaxed stance and Sam’s facial and hair silhouette.
