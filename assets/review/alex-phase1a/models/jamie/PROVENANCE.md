## Component history

| Component | Treatment in this checkpoint |
|---|---|
| Body, ears, hands, feet, facial surface | Derived from Child. Import seams welded, compatible triangle pairs rejoined, proportions and stance reshaped. The supplied UV layout is preserved on the body. A reversible mask covers anatomy under the summer wardrobe. |
| Eye sockets and lips | Source lid and lip topology retained. The inner eye-surface geometry informed the new ocular components. The mouth, cheeks, nose, jaw and eyelid shape were edited. |
| Eyes, brows, lashes and skin finish | New globe geometry, curved optical surfaces, iris colour, brow and lash geometry, facial colour, roughness and limb colour. |
| Hair, cap and clothing | New geometry and materials. The garments, groom and cap were fitted to the revised anatomy. |
| Textures, lights, environments and renders | Authored for this checkpoint. All review images are Blender Cycles renders of the editable scene. |

The original GLB contained one material, one UV set and no image textures. The supplied GLB is included byte-for-byte for recovery and attribution.

## Targeted Phase 1A revision

The revised source scene changes Jamie’s resting shoulders, arms and fingers and fits his shirt to the revised stance. These are further modifications to the credited anatomical source. The new matched renders use both revised scenes. The original GLB is shared at the top level of this package.

## Phase 1A correction pass 2

The arm cage and hand contours were reconstructed, and the orange T-shirt and khaki shorts were recut and refitted on Jamie’s saved character. The original anatomical GLB is shared in the source folder of this package. The studio and clay views were rendered in Blender Cycles.
