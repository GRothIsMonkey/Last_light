# Alex · Source and attribution

Roth Studios’ Last Light character Alex adapts the anatomical mesh from *Child* by gelmi.com.br, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The original file is included at `../../source/child.glb`. [Original model](https://skfb.ly/oLutp) · [Creator](https://sketchfab.com/rodrigogelmi) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The supplied GLB is 517,152 bytes. Its SHA-256 is `42bb1684d20195e5d9e9b5a692e87b5afbc6d5f003129493743d1bcb10131d38`.

The Last Light studio workflow supplied the corrected neutral arm anatomy, garment block, footwear construction and review lighting from the Jamie and Sam checkpoint. For Alex, the production changes include new craniofacial proportions, eyelids, brows, hazel eyes, complexion, freckles, a listening smile, a balanced stance, a swept native-curve groom, a gold polo with a separate fitted yoke and folded collar, olive shorts and slate canvas shoes. The character’s studio and exterior views were rendered in Blender Cycles.
