# envrepair.kit — provenance

**Original work for Last Light**, built by `tools/blender/envrepair/build_envrepair_kit.py` on the details kit's own helpers,
piece frames and atlas (`tools/blender/details/build_details.py`, `assets/source/details/textures/`): the trampoline, swing set,
big wheel and wagon rebuilt with corrected supports, feet, hangers, seat thickness and moulded shapes. No third-party geometry
or texture. The creek repair places the vegetation benchmark's own library (`veg.shrubs`, `veg.ground`, `veg.street-trees`,
credited in `docs/THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`); its soft ground patches and contact shadows are generated in code at runtime
(`dist/envrepair.js`). Files: `envrepair.kit.blend`, `build-stats.json`, `dist/assets/envrepair/envrepair.kit.glb`.
