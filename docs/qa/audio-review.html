Last Light · audio review

These are actual offline browser renders of the game's synthesized sounds, at the game's output levels, including the Chapter 1 additions. Start at a comfortable headphone volume. No listening verdict has been recorded: nobody on this pass has listened to them.

Check for harshness, obvious synthetic artifacts, distracting repetition and abrupt cuts. For Chapter 1: whether the siren reads as a real siren far off and near, whether the radio and TV read as voices through a speaker and a wall, and whether the dad's call stays ordinary and distant. The siren's Doppler sweep and the bell at the end only happen in play; these excerpts are short.
