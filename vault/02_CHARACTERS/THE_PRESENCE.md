> handoff: "The darkness that takes the lights is never shown or named (in code it is *the Presence*, `dist/presence.js`),
> and has no model." Chapter Five's code: "In the code it is the Presence again… here it has a camp to work with."

## Rules of portrayal (keep)

1. **Never a body, a model or a face.** It is felt through lights, screens, voices, stillness and what the creature does.
2. **Never named or explained** on screen. No line may imply a hierarchy ("controls", "servant", "in charge", "created" —
   checked by Chapter Four's tests).
3. **Ordered, not random.** Its light cascades are deterministic: ordered by distance along a path at a set speed.
4. **It follows the player's real choices** (the trail they walked, the light they blinked, where they sat).
5. **It escalates** (human direction for the remaining chapters).
