# Jamie

> ✅ CONFIRMED CANON for Chapters 0–4; 🟡 for Chapter Five details.

## Personality

**Impulsive, funny, loyal; his confidence cracks.** (Chapter Five handoff: "Jamie is impulsive, funny and loyal, and his
confidence cracks.") He moves first, jokes under pressure, refuses to leave Alex, and is the one the camp copies.

In Chapter Five's companion AI (`PERSON.jamie`): leads (walks a little ahead), reacts faster (0.35–0.85 s), gets curious
sooner and about **water, climbing and odd things**; looks back every 6–10 s; gestures: hands on hips, shifting weight,
looking around. Walk 1.45 m/s, run 3.9 m/s.

## Look (`CAST.jamie`)

Blue **cap** over dark-brown hair; an easy **grin**; orange short-sleeved T-shirt; khaki shorts; white socks and shoes;
slightly sturdier build. Rides a red-brown **BMX** with BMX bars, **pegs and a pad**; stands on the pedals more than the
others, weaves, glances around, answers a surge fastest. In Chapter Five he wears a **backpack**.

## Home and family

Two-story house on Oak Hollow with a **red porch chair**, a planter and a stepping-stone path down the bedroom side; his
side bedroom window is how you wake him (Ch1). His **mother** calls him in at the end of the ride and comes to the door
(Ch0); she made him late in Ch5 ("Sorry. Sorry, sorry. My mom."); she keeps saying "when he comes home," not "if" (Ch5).
