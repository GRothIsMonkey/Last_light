# Alex

> ✅ CONFIRMED CANON for Chapters 0–4; 🟡 for Chapter Five details. Alex is the missing friend. He appears in person only
> in the Prologue and in Chapter Two's memory; everything after is evidence, recordings, photographs, or something that
> wears his face.

## Facts

- **No surname** in the game (so "AR" can never be his initials). — Ch1 rule
- **12 years old** (police radio). — Ch1
- Lives on **Briarwood Lane**, just past the creek, upstairs front-corner bedroom over the garage. — Ch1, Ch3
- Last seen around **8 PM, Aug 21, 2011**, on Oak Hollow, riding a green bicycle. — Ch1
- Heard a bicycle bell outside at night for several nights (Thu–Sat) and asked his mom about it; recorded it on Saturday
  08/20 at 11:52 PM: **"There it is again."** — Ch3
- Never rides without his helmet ("His mom makes him"); it was left on his desk. — Ch3
- Left his backpack, with his camera, in Sam's family garage on Saturday. — Ch4
- Photographed the old bike in the background of four pictures, the last outside Mason Cycle & Sport the Wednesday
  before. — Ch4
- 🟡 Once fished off the Pine Ridge dock for three hours and caught a shoe he named **Gary**; keeps it on the trophy shelf
  in his garage ("It's his best trophy."). Could skip a stone "like ten" (Jamie). — Ch5
- 🟡 His **gold shirt** ("the one from the flyers") hung wet in Owl cabin on Aug 24. — Ch5

## Look (`CAST.alex`)

Swept light-brown hair; green-brown eyes; a smile; a **mustard/gold collared shirt** with cream trim; **olive/dark-green
shorts**; dark shoes. Rides an olive-green **cruiser** with swept bars, a brown saddle, cream grips, a **rack**, a **bell**
and a **red rear reflector** (`REFLECTOR` constant shared with the broken creek piece). Helmet: **red, white stripe, his
number**. Phone: a small flip phone with a voice recorder. Camera: a cheap 2009 point-and-shoot (silver front, black
back, 2.7-inch screen, wrist strap).

## His last evening (Prologue)

"Even when school starts?" · "Did you guys hear that?" … "Never mind." · "You'll still live here next summer, right?" ·
"Alright, I'm this way. See you tomorrow." Then: Briarwood, the bell twice, a wave over his shoulder, gone round the curve.
In Chapter Two's memory you see what nobody noticed: **he all but stops for about 3.4 s with his head turned toward the
creek woods** before waving and riding on.

## His room (Chapter Three)

An ordinary 2011 boy's bedroom: rumpled bed, nightstand with a red LED clock, desk under the front window (notebook,
pencils, game cases, lamp, coin jar), dresser with a small CRT and console, a **box fan** by the side window, clothes,
sneakers, the **red helmet**, comics, a basketball, posters (space shuttle LIFTOFF, BMX RIDE, a band, an **August 2011
calendar**), a corkboard. The side window looks over the garage roof to the trees: the creek. See `dist/alex-room.js`.

## Things that are not Alex

The boy in the drain, at the gangway, under the marquee and in the road looks and sounds like him. "That wasn't Alex."
See [The Alex-like figures](THE_ALEX_LIKE_FIGURES.md).

## Family

**Mom**: on the porch with a cordless phone (Ch1); tired and polite; "I keep thinking he's going to come around that
corner."; she told him the bell was the neighbor kids and to go to sleep (Ch3). **Dad**: in the driveway with the
officer, "He never came home."; calls "Alex!" down the street; comes to the easement and makes a restrained bracing
gesture (Ch2).
