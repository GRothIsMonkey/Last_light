# The Alex-like figures and Jamie's double

> Figures that look and sound like the boys' friends but are not. "That wasn't Alex." Never explained. Whether they are
> the Presence, the creature, or something else is ❓ UNKNOWN.

## The false Alex — ✅ CONFIRMED CANON

**Appearance.** The same person build as Alex: his height and proportions, his **mustard shirt**, dark-green shorts,
swept brown hair, at normal scale, standing on the ground. No glow, no distortion. Seen at 20–25 m in flashlight beams,
his face is not readable. In the drain he "catches the light" a little more than he should when far off in a beam.

| 4 | Video store phone | A voice only | "…Jamie?" |

**Rules.** He waits to be seen. He never runs at them. He is never on screen with the creature. He **repeats words that
were really said** — Alex's on the hill, and **Sam's own "Don't run. Don't run." from Second Street, where the figure
was not**. Nothing about him threatens, smiles or distorts.

**Related historic sighting:** in September 1991, a boy matching Danny Whitcomb was seen on the Mill Street bridge at dusk;
"He looked at me, and then he walked off toward the creek." (Ch4 microfilm). "People saw him. After." "Like we did."

## Jamie's double — 🟡 IMPLEMENTED BUT UNAPPROVED (Chapter Five)

**Appearance.** Jamie: his own clothes and his own cap, Jamie's face (built from Jamie's cast entry; `doubleWalk` in
`dist/chapter5-presence.js`). It is smiling with Jamie's face.

**Sequence.** From Owl's porch, with the real Jamie beside you and Owl's lamp on: the double stands where Alex's shirt
hung. It turns its head to look at Jamie. "…That's my hat." "That's me." Sam: "Jamie. Look at me. Don't look at it." Then
it **comes out** through the door, across the porch, toward the three of you, at a walk (8.1 m to 1.4 m in the test). "Back
up. Back UP." Only when it is close does every light in the camp come on, and Sam says **RUN**. It is gone when you look
back.

**Status.** The human developer judged the escape that follows it ineffective; Astra's redesign (in progress) makes the
pursuer follow the path the player actually walks (observed in Astra's branch; unapproved). See
[Chapter Five Astra objectives](../08_PLAYTESTING_AND_ISSUES/CHAPTER5_ASTRA_OBJECTIVES.md).
