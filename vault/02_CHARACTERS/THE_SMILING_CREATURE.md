# The smiling creature

> ✅ CONFIRMED CANON (Chapters Three and Four); 🟡 in Chapter Five. **Never named in the game.** The boys call it "the
> thing in the tunnel" / "the thing from the drain" ("It's the thing. From the tunnel."). In code and docs it is "the
> creature". How it moves and is staged: [Smiling creature behavior](../05_HORROR_DESIGN/SMILING_CREATURE_BEHAVIOR.md).

## What it looks like

"A pale, thin, long-armed humanoid with a wide red grin of teeth and hollow eyes", crouched **on all fours** (head about
0.75 m up at the model's own scale; in game scaled ×1.35: about 1.3 m long and 1.3 m to the shoulders). Its design was
not changed for the game.

**Asset:** "Smily horror monster" by **Bento** (https://skfb.ly/6W6ut), **CC BY 4.0**. Original kept byte for byte in
`assets/source/creature/smily_horror_monster.glb` (never loaded); runtime copy `dist/assets/creature/creature.glb`
(textures re-encoded as JPEG; geometry, skeleton and animation unchanged). Attribution:
[docs/THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md](../../docs/THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md). The game's Credits screen does not yet name the author
(fix before public release).

## What is established about it

- It has **never touched anyone**. Standing still in the Chapter Three chase, it stops about 4.6 m away and rears.
- It is **afraid of the Presence** (Ch4) and of the Presence's lights (Ch5). "It was scared." "Of what?"
- The false Alex **led the boys to it** (Ch3). The two are **never on screen together**.
- In 1991 a "pale person crouched in the storm culvert under the Mill Street bridge" was reported with a bell ringing
  (Ch4 microfilm). Whether that was this creature: ❓ UNKNOWN.
