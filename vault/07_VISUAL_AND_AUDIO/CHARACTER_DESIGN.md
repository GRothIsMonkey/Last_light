## Known deficiencies (from the Chapter Five Astra prep handoff, for the current visual pass)

- **Rig and proportions:** tube limbs with ball shoulders (read as pauldrons), seams at elbows and wrists, long thin neck,
  long forearms, bulbous pelvis, oversized head; knock-kneed rest poses.
- **Clothing:** rigid tube shirts, a separate torus collar, a hem ring that reads as "a glass hula-hoop", plain cylinder
  shorts; Jamie's tee has a seam that reads as "a second mouth".
- **Shoes:** a flat white sole plate overhangs "like a ski"; no contact shadow.
- **Hands:** blocky fists; nothing gripped by fingers.
- **Faces:** flat disc eyelids, faceted eyes with oversized pupils and no iris, cone noses; mouths small and low contrast;
  expression range too subtle for a laugh, surprise or relief.
- **Hair:** solid helmets with a sawtooth fringe.

Earlier chapters' art passes improved hair, blinks, garments and heads (Prologue, Chapter Three); those are preserved.
