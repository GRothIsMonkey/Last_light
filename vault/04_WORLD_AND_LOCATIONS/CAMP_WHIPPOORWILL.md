Enterable: the office, the lodge, Bluejay, Cardinal and Owl (Owl only from dusk; "It's stuck. Swollen shut. Leave it.").
Walls, tables, benches and logs are solid (353 navigation solids in the first draft). Doors block until they have
visibly swung open.

the trees, the three lights, the creature by the lodge, the split, the double, the run. See
[Chapter Five](../03_CHAPTERS/CHAPTER_5_THE_PLACE_IN_THE_PICTURE.md).

## Known visual problems (Astra)

Flat untextured walls; plastic-looking posts; black porch ceilings; buildings evenly lit with no visible source; the camp
gate has blank posts and **no fence line** ("a slammed gate that could be walked round. A fence into the woods would make
the slam a threat."); the porch light is "a bare white egg on an invisible cord". See
[Chapter Five Astra objectives](../08_PLAYTESTING_AND_ISSUES/CHAPTER5_ASTRA_OBJECTIVES.md).
