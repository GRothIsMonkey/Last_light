## 1. The creek strip (Chapter One)

"What the kids call the concrete storm channel that crosses under Briarwood Lane just before its curve." A strip of
trees and weeds between two lots, chain-link along both sides: the concrete channel, **culvert headwalls and a railing**
at the road, a grate wall, a worn dirt path down the inside bank, and **a loose gap in the back chain-link** leading on
into the dark. The bikes are left by the culvert railing.

- **Clue:** Alex's broken red reflector with a black tape band, in the weeds by the channel; an optional faint tire line.
- **The bell** (Ch1) sounds from the woods beyond the fence gap.
- Fireflies; water and katydid sounds.

## 2. The drainage easement and the culvert (Chapter Two)

Through the widened gap (about 1.85 m): a corridor behind the yards. A concrete **V channel** with a trickle of water, an
**outfall headwall**, a dirt path on the left bank, a power line overhead, about 420 weed tufts, brush and 16 trees, and a
**big box culvert** set into a wooded rise (headwall, cap, wingwalls, dark interior, standing water, storm debris).
"Neighborhood kids know it is there and nobody else thinks about it."

| Distance from the gap | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 9.2 m | Mud with one narrow **tire track** |
| 17 m | Waist-high weeds with **one lane pressed flat** toward the channel |
| 25.8 m | **Scrape** marks and rubber down the concrete lip |
| By the culvert | **Alex's bike**, on its side at the top of the bank |
| ~19 m inside the culvert (s = 52) | Where **the bell** rings, twice (muffled, with echoes) |

The culvert is walkable only about a step inside its mouth. Bikes stay on the street side of the fence. Police tape later
goes round the bike on four stakes. In daylight (morning) the bike is gone.

## 3. The stormwater access road (Chapter Three)

From the end of Briarwood: a new streetlight, bollards, a red **END** marker, a yellow **pipe gate** (one leaf open, a
chain), Public Works "MAINTENANCE ACCESS" and flood-warning signs, a sewer marker post. Then about **547.5 m** of road into
the woods, descending about 10 m, narrowing from 5.5 m to 3.9 m:

| Stage | Distance | Surface | What happens at night |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 0–150 m | Cracked, patched asphalt; dead power lines | At the bend (118 m) the houses drop out of sight |
| Middle | 150–330 m | Broken asphalt; "ROAD NOT MAINTAINED" at 300 m; trees close overhead | A branch swings back behind you; a delineator reflector flares; something crosses between the trees |
| Deep | 330–547 m | Gravel and ruts | Down into the creek valley ("I can hear water."); the outfall |

By day only the first stretch is open (gate, tire track, small shoeprints, weeds pushed down; no old bike).
About 470 trees near the road and 3,700 far trees.

## 4. The outfall (Chapter Three)

Where "the creek from behind Alex's house comes out" ("That's where the creek goes. Behind Alex's. All of it comes out
here."). A 0.45 m concrete headwall round a **4.6 × 3.6 m** black mouth, a cap beam, sloped wingwalls 6.3 m long, stains
and a waterline, a rusted rail, a 9.2 m apron with a trickle, riprap and debris, a gravel pad where the bikes are left.

## 5. The storm drain (Chapter Three)

**286 m on foot, one way in, never a maze, no changing geometry.**

| s (m) | Section | Size (w × h) | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–72 | Mouth and first box | 4.6 × 3.6 | Big portal; raised walkway; graffiti and station stencils; the outside light behind you |
| 72–94 | First bend | 4.4 × 3.3 | **The way out leaves your sight.** The old maintenance **gate** here is slammed during the chase. |
| 94–150 | Old section | 3.7 × 2.7 | Older concrete, flood lines, cracks, roots; side drains; a rusted ladder up a shaft (121); the silt bar with shoeprints and a tire line; a side pipe (136) that bursts during the run; Alex's helmet (141) |
| 150–226 | Deep box | 3.1 × 2.3 | Ankle-deep water; a narrow dry ledge; spalled concrete with rebar; pipes overhead; a ladder to a sealed hatch (187); side openings (176, 194 — the creature comes out of 194); the old bike (163) |
| 226–286 | Far stretch and junction | 3.2 × 2.4, then 5.2 × 3.5 | Where the false Alex is first seen (~212) and walks round the bend; the junction with its **side culvert** (Briarwood's own pipe) where he climbs in and the creature appears |

"No blood, writing, symbols, bones, dolls or eyes. It is ordinary neglected infrastructure." Deep inside, only the
tunnel is drawn (69 merged meshes) and the sky is not.

## How the pieces connect

The creek crosses under Briarwood → its water continues through the easement culvert → the storm sewer trunk → the
outfall in the woods at the end of the access road. Jamie and Mr. Okafor both say the creek ends up at the big storm
drain. Whether the easement culvert is physically modeled as connected to the trunk is **not yet verified** (the game
presents them as one system).
