# Oak Hollow

> ✅ CONFIRMED CANON. A fictional American suburban township in summer 2011. Its paper is *The Oak Hollow Courier*; its
> bicycle licences read "OAK HOLLOW TWP."; its police have a chief (Hollis, 1966) and an Officer Reyes (2011).

## The feel (art direction)

A grounded, warm, lived-in 2011 suburb: lap-sided ranches, colonials, capes and front-gable houses; driveways with
parked cars; basketball hoops; sprinklers; bins, hoses, trampolines, kiddie pools, big wheels; power lines on poles;
streetlights that come on at dusk. Beyond the playable streets a whole neighborhood continues (106 second-row houses,
637 simplified far houses, horizon trees) so no glance finds the edge of the world. See
[Environment design](../07_VISUAL_AND_AUDIO/ENVIRONMENT_DESIGN.md).

## The town's history (as far as the game shows)

1958 Mason Cycle & Sport opens on Main; 1966 Ruth Ann Carver disappears riding to the Lyric; 1988 Mason's 30th
anniversary; 1991 Danny Whitcomb disappears from Mill Street, a pale figure is reported in the Mill Street culvert,
streetlights fail on Main; 1999 Roy Mason dies and the shop closes. See [Historic cases](../01_STORY_BIBLE/HISTORIC_CASES.md).

## Rules of the world (gameplay)

- Everything is reachable by bike or on foot; there are no invisible walls in story areas, only natural limits (a police
  officer, a fence, the edge of a property).
- Each chapter opens only the parts it needs: Chapter Four "opens" Summerfield and the town in navigation
  (`nav.setTown`), Chapter Five opens the road past the sawhorse (`nav.setPine`). Earlier chapters' reachable world is
  unchanged.
