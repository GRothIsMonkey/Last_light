## Alex's room (Chapter Three)

Built as real geometry inside his house (`dist/alex-room.js`), walkable in first person: bed with rumpled comforter;
nightstand with a red LED clock; desk under the front window (chair, notebook, pencils, game cases, lamp, coin jar, the
**flip phone**); dresser with a small CRT and console; **box fan** by the side window; clothes, sneakers, backpack;
the **red helmet**; comics; a basketball; posters (space shuttle LIFTOFF, BMX RIDE, a band, an **August 2011 calendar**);
corkboard. The side window looks over the garage roof toward the trees and the creek. Outdoor sound is muffled inside.
In Chapter Four, footage on the video-store TV shows **Alex in his bed at night from the corner of his ceiling**.

## Jamie's and Sam's windows (Chapter One)

Both friends' bedrooms have working "sneak windows" with lit rooms. AC units were moved off the bedroom walls so they
don't sit under the windows. Prompts appear within about 2.5 m when facing the right side; no arrows or markers.
