## Geography

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Oak Hollow Drive** | The main street of the ride. The prologue route is about 1,120 m from the start to the cul-de-sac; the street also runs about 430 m *behind* the start so looking back never finds an edge. Signs: SLOW CHILDREN AT PLAY, SPEED LIMIT 25, **NO OUTLET** past Summerfield. |
| **Briarwood Lane** | Cross street to the **right** at about 595 m. About 250 m long, bending over a crest (a 0.9 rad bend from u 62 over 50 m), crossing the **creek** just before its curve. **Alex's house** is just round the curve (u 127). The far end (u ≈ 250) has a streetlight, bollards, an END marker and a gate to the **stormwater access road** (Ch3). |
| **Summerfield Road** | Cross street to the **left** at about 870 m. In Chapter Four the boys ride all of it, then on to Old Mill Road and town. |
| Junctions | Corner posts with stop signs and double-sided street-name blades; 6.5 m curb returns; stop bars. |
| **The cul-de-sac** | Where Oak Hollow Drive ends. |
| **The lookout** | A small grassy rise at the end: a **bench**, the **old oak** (a 14-sided trunk with roots) with a **tire swing** on 3.35 m of rope, **chalk** initials (JSA; faint AR) and a chalk drawing of riders on the pavement, **AR carved in the oak's bark** at a kid's height facing the swing, and a fence at the edge of a field. |
| Power lines | One connected network: 114 pole-to-pole spans, service drops to the houses. |
| Life | Sprinklers, a kid shooting hoops, a minivan coming home down Briarwood, birds, fireflies, porch lights and streetlights coming on one by one. |

## The old oak (story significance)

The friends' meeting place: comparing memories (Ch1), "Oak. Tomorrow morning." (Ch2), the old bike appearing and
vanishing (Ch0–2), the end of Chapter Four ("That thing in the tunnel…" "It was scared." "Of what?"). JSA is chalked
there; AR is carved there.
