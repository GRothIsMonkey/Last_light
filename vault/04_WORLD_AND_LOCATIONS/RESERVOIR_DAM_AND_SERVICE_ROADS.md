## The reservoir

The lake below the ridge, managed by the **Reservoir Authority** (their gray shed is at the old gravel lot). The park is
on its shore; the camp is along the north shore; the dam is at the west end. In 1991 volunteers searched "the shoreline
of the Pine Ridge reservoir" for Danny Whitcomb (✅ Ch4). The water is a single designed level (`WATER` in
`pine-plan.js`), with **wade zones** at the beach and lawn where the bed is under water.

## The service road and the dam

"Behind it all the service yard, the pump house and the service road that goes round the west end of the lake over the
dam and back along the south shore to the picnic lawn." About **456 m**: service yard → pump house → **the dam** (a
walkway) → the south shore → the picnic lawn. Walkable.

## Story use: escape route C

After the double and "RUN.", Sam: "The dam! It's open, it's open, go!" Through the service yard, behind the pump house,
onto the service road and over the dam. **The dinner bell rings by itself** back across the water: "Nobody's there.
Nobody's at the bell." Then the walk back along the south shore to the bikes, **about 300 m from the dam** — part of the
"excessively long" escape the human developer criticised. In the repair, a player still getting nearer is never cut short;
one who stops for 90 s (or after 8 minutes) is faded to the rack.

Route A (the road) instead goes back through the camp to the gate and along the 190 m gravel road.
