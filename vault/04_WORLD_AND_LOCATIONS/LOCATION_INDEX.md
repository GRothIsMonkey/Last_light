# Location index

The world is one connected, physically continuous place: you can ride from the prologue's cul-de-sac to downtown and
on to Pine Ridge without a loading screen. All of it is generated at runtime from data files (`dist/layout.js`,
`dist/town-plan.js`, `dist/pine-plan.js`).

## How the places connect

```text
                    N
   Pine Ridge reservoir ── Camp Whippoorwill (north shore) ── service road ── dam ── south shore
        │                         │ gravel road (190 m)
   Pine Ridge Park ── old gravel lot (brown sign, yellow gate, gray shed)
        │
   Old Reservoir Road (over the wooded ridge, past a farm)   ~"four miles" from town (Sam)
        │
   Mill Street, past its LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY sawhorse
        │
   DOWNTOWN OAK HOLLOW (Main St; Mill, Second, Depot; the square; Mill Creek)
        │
   Old Mill Road (~630 m, over a rise: water tower, steeple)
        │
   Summerfield Road ── Oak Hollow Drive ── Briarwood Lane ── the creek strip ── Alex's house ── end of Briarwood
        │                 │                     │                                                    │
   (left at 870 m)   the cul-de-sac          the easement & culvert (behind the yards)        stormwater access road
                     & the lookout                                                              (~550 m into the woods)
                     (old oak)                                                                        │
                                                                                     the outfall ── 286 m storm drain
```

*(Schematic only, not to scale. The vault's reading of the plans; directions other than "north to Pine Ridge" are not
verified.)*
