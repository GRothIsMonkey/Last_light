## Owl in detail

- Approached from the west end of the camp lane; a porch the boys stand on at night ("We look. From the porch. We don't
  go in.").
- The carving: "J. S. A." "Like at the lookout. Like he wrote in chalk." "It's new. The wood's still white." "Four
  marks." "Sunday. Monday. Tuesday. Today." "And an arrow. Pointing… that way."
- The shirt: "That's Alex's. That's the one from the flyers." "It's wet." "It's still wet."
- Fallbacks if the player does not go: after 120 s Jamie leads ("Owl's the last one. Down here."); after 240 s a fade
  brings the player to the porch (first-draft figures; the repair keeps "the boys pull you along" first).
- Known issue: Sam can linger inside Owl after taking the shirt in some runs; Sam's stand point by the nail once had room
  only for a 0.1 m body (fixed in the final audit).
