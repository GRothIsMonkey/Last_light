# Psychological horror

The game's deepest fears are not the creature: they are that the familiar is wrong, that you are watched, that your
memory and your friends can be copied, and that nobody will believe you.

## The techniques in use

| Technique | Examples | Label |
|---|---|---|
| **The familiar made wrong** | Alex's face and voice on something that is not Alex (Ch3–4); Jamie's double (Ch5); Sam's mother's voice (Ch5) | ✅ / 🟡 |
| **Your own words used against you** | The marquee figure repeats Alex's words and **Sam's words from where it was not** (Ch4) | ✅ |
| **Being watched** | Live TV pictures from impossible angles (Ch4); a camera that moves when you approach (Ch4); a photograph taken of you from the trees (Ch5) | ✅ / 🟡 |
| **Being copied** | Three lights retrace your afternoon, copy your flashlight, sit where you sat (Ch5) | 🟡 |
| **Unreliable memory** | "He stopped first." "No he didn't." (Ch1) → the memory proves Sam right (Ch2); "Wasn't there an old bike here last night?" "What old bike?" (Ch2); "I thought I remembered everyone." (Ch0) | ✅ |
| **Things that change when unobserved** | The old bike; the fifth chalk rider; figures in windows that vanish when looked at | ✅ |
| **Separation** | The camp pulls at whichever friend you leave; "Jamie?" calls one of them away | 🟡 |
| **Not being believed** | "We heard a bell. In there." "Okay." (Ch2) | ✅ |
| **History repeating** | 1966, 1991, 2011, the same days of August ("That's today." "Twenty years ago today.") | ✅ |
| **Hope as pain** | "Maybe he couldn't." (Ch5); Jamie's mother saying "when" | 🟡 |
| **Isolation** | The drain's bend hides the way out ("I can't see the way out anymore."); "Nobody knows we're here." | ✅ / 🟡 |

## Rules

1. **Keep it personal.** Generic threats ("I know where he is. Come find him.") were rejected in favour of the boys' own
   words. Reuse what the player has heard and done.
2. **Never explain the trick.** The player should be able to trace *what* was copied, never *how* or *why*.
3. **Let the companions notice.** "That's what he said. On the hill. That's exactly—" / "It's doing what you do. Exactly
   what you do." The realization is shared out loud.
4. **Pair it with physical threat** now (2026-10-23 mandate): the copy that walks toward you is scarier than the copy that
   stands still.
5. **Respect the friendship.** The horror attacks the bonds (separation, imitation) — it should hurt because the bonds are
   real.
