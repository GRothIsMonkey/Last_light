# Design direction for the remaining chapters

> 💡 **PROPOSED direction**, stated by the human developer in the vault brief (2026-10-23). It describes *how the rest of
> the game should feel*, not what happens. It does not lock any event, survivor, death, sacrifice or ending.

## The direction

1. **Increasingly frightening.** Each remaining chapter should be more frightening than the last. *Last Light* is a
   genuine horror game: meaningful jumpscares, terrifying supernatural encounters, physical danger, psychological horror,
   atmospheric dread, effective chases, and emotional storytelling.
2. **Back to familiar suburban places.** The story should return to the neighborhood and town the player already knows
   (Oak Hollow, Briarwood, the creek, the friends' houses, Main Street), so that the familiar becomes unsafe.
3. **The Presence escalates.** Its influence should grow: what it can reach, copy and take.
4. **The friendship stays central.** Jamie, Sam and the player (and Alex, absent) remain the heart of the story.
5. **Jumpscares and physical danger matter.** Threats must be perceivable and must be able to reach the boys (how far
   that goes, including whether the player can be caught, is undecided).
6. **An emotionally powerful ending.**
7. **Nothing final is locked** unless the human developer confirms it: no final survivor, death, sacrifice or ending.

## What the established game already gives this direction (facts)

| Direction | Existing material it can build on (with source) |
|---|---|
| Suburbia turned unsafe | Briarwood, the creek strip, the easement, the storm drain under the neighborhood (Ch1–3); Main Street's lights (Ch4) |
| Presence escalation so far | Ch2–3: a bell with no ringer; Ch3: a figure with Alex's face and voice; Ch4: the town's lights taken in cascades, live impossible TV pictures, a phone call, figures in windows, a figure repeating their own words; Ch5 (unapproved): lights that copy them, voices of their families, the camp pulling them apart, a double of Jamie, a photograph taken with Alex's camera |
| Physical danger | Ch3's chase (the creature never touches anyone; closest about 4.6–6 m); Ch4's dark following the player's trail; Ch5's double walking toward them |
| Friendship | Jamie's impulsiveness and cracking confidence; Sam's preparation and dry kindness; their disagreements (the yellow gate) and compromises ("Together.") |
| Emotional core | "I thought I remembered everyone."; "You moved away in October. You meant to keep in touch."; Jamie's mother saying "when," not "if" |

## Constraints that come from canon

- The Presence and the creature have **never been named** on screen.
- The creature has so far **never harmed anyone** and has been **afraid** of the Presence (Ch4, Ch5).
- The false Alex and the creature are **never on screen together** (Ch3 rule).
- Alex has **no surname**; "AR" is not Alex.
- The adults have **not believed** the boys (Ch2: "We heard a bell. In there." "Okay.").
- The player **moves away in October 2011** (prologue narration). Whatever happens, the narrator survives to look back —
  unless the human developer decides that narration means something else. ❓ UNKNOWN how it is meant.
