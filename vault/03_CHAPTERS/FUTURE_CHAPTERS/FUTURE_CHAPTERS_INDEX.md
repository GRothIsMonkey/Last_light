## Before any future chapter starts

1. Chapter Five should be approved by the human developer (or the dependencies on it listed and accepted as risk).
2. The human decides the chapter's premise. An AI may draft **options**, labeled 💡 PROPOSED, but does not pick.
3. Use [the proposal template](../../11_TEMPLATES/FUTURE_CHAPTER_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md), then the
   [chapter template](../../11_TEMPLATES/CHAPTER_NOTE_TEMPLATE.md) once it is being built.
4. Run the [Continuity checklist](../../10_AI_DEVELOPMENT_GUIDES/CONTINUITY_CHECKLIST.md).
