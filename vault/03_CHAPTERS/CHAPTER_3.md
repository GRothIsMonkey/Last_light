## Setting

By day: the Briarwood corner, **Alex's house** and **his room** (upstairs front corner over the garage), his street and
neighbours, and the **end of Briarwood**, where a gate opens onto an old municipal **stormwater access road** into the
woods. By night: that road (about 550 m, descending about 10 m into the creek valley), the **outfall** where the creek
from behind Alex's house comes out, and a **286 m storm drain** walked on foot. See
[Creek and drainage](../04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/CREEK_AND_DRAINAGE.md).

## Starting conditions

A few minutes after Chapter Two, at the corner where Alex turned. "If Alex heard it before he left…" — "Maybe yesterday
wasn't the first time." — "His mom would know."

## Characters

You, Jamie, Sam; **Alex's mom**; neighbours **Mr. Okafor** (the one who matters), Mr. Huang, Mrs. Delaney, Mr. Pruitt
(a line each if asked); the **Alex-like boy**; **the creature**. See [Adult characters](../02_CHARACTERS/ADULT_CHARACTERS.md),
[The Alex-like figures](../02_CHARACTERS/THE_ALEX_LIKE_FIGURES.md), [The smiling creature](../02_CHARACTERS/THE_SMILING_CREATURE.md).

## Story progression — the day

1. **Alex's mom** (about 30 s; she speaks first). Tired and polite. "He kept asking if I heard a bike bell outside. At
   night." Thursday, Friday, maybe Saturday. She thought it was a neighbour kid. She lets them up to his room.
2. **His room.** An ordinary 2011 boy's bedroom you walk in yourself (bed, desk under the window, small CRT and console,
   box fan, posters, an August 2011 calendar, a red LED clock). **His red bike helmet** with a white stripe and his
   number: Sam: "His helmet's still here." Jamie: "He never rides without it. His mom makes him." "His phone. The cops
   went through it and gave it back."
3. **The flip phone's voice recorder** opens on the recording that matters: **Saturday 08/20, 11:52 PM**: his box fan,
   insects, a bike bell outside, the bell again, and Alex, quietly: **"There it is again."** Then the bed creaks,
   footsteps, the blinds. Four ordinary older recordings are optional (Jamie and Sam cracking up; a freewheel ticking,
   "That's my new ringtone"; the TV downstairs; crickets, "Summer night. Very exciting."). "'There it is again.' He'd
   heard it before."
4. **The window** looks over the garage roof toward the trees: the creek. "Somebody else on his street had to hear it.
   Mr. Okafor's up all night."
5. **Mr. Okafor** heard the bell too, a few nights, late, toward the end of the street: "End of Briarwood, past the last
   house. There's a gate. An old city road goes down from there. Down to the big storm drain in the woods. Your creek
   ends up down there." "Nobody's used it in years. You boys stay off it."
6. **The road by day.** At the end of Briarwood: a streetlight, bollards, a red END marker, a yellow pipe gate with one
   leaf open, Public Works and flood signs. A **single bike tire track** wobbling along the edge with **small
   shoeprints** beside it ("Somebody walked it in."), **weeds pushed down** ("That's from today. Or last night."). The
   road bends down into the trees and the street goes quiet. Sam stops them. **No old bike by day.**
7. **The plan.** "We have to come back tonight." Sam doesn't want to; agrees only if "If anything happens, we leave.
   Right away." **"Eleven. The corner. Bring a flashlight."**

## Story progression — the night

1. **Out at 11:12 PM.** Jamie and Sam at the corner ("You came."). Down the access road on bikes with a handlebar light:
   the houses drop out of sight; a branch swings back behind you; a reflector flares ("Reflector. Relax."); something
   crosses between the trees ("Deer." "Since when are there deer here?"); the creek valley.
2. **The outfall.** A concrete headwall around a black 4.6 × 3.6 m mouth. "That's where the creek goes. Behind Alex's."
   Sam: "We are not going in there." Jamie: "The tracks went this way." **Bikes stay at the mouth; the drain is on foot.**
3. **The drain closes in.** Round the first bend the way out is gone ("I can't see the way out anymore."). Small
   shoeprints and one tire line in the silt, going in. A knock up a ladder shaft. **One bell far ahead.**
4. **His helmet**, upside down in the silt, deep in. "…That's his helmet." "That was on his desk. This morning." Sam turns
   to face the way out: "We need to tell somebody. Right now." Jamie: "Just to the end. Then we go."
5. **The old bike from the oak**, upright against the wall, found by the light from about 15 m. "That's the bike. From
   the oak." Sam: "I see it. I see it this time." F: "The license sticker. Four-one-seven." "It's wet. It wasn't wet at
   the oak." F, try the bell: **a dry click, no ring**. A clear bell, farther in.
6. **The deep box** (3.1 × 2.3 m, ankle-deep water, a dry ledge). Well past the bike, it is **gone** (never seen moving).
7. **The boy.** Jamie and Sam stop either side of you, both lights down the tunnel. About **22 m** away, a boy Alex's size
   in **his mustard shirt and dark green shorts**, facing them. **He waits until you have actually looked at him** (cues,
   never a turned camera; 40 s fallback). "…Alex?" He turns slowly and walks round the bend.
8. **Follow.** "Alex! ALEX! Wait!" Past the bend: nobody; a wet print on the ledge, a hanging cable still swinging.
9. **The lure.** At the far junction he stands by a black side culvert **with his back to them**. "ALEX! It's us! It's
   Jamie!" He walks to the culvert, climbs over its sill and goes in. From inside, his voice: **"Jamie?"** Jamie: "Alex?
   …Alex, come out. It's okay." (You slow as you near it; you cannot reach it.)
10. **The creature.** Something comes up to the **lip of the culvert**, head and arms out over the sill, and **waits until
    you have seen it**. "…That's not—" **"That's not Alex."** It drops into the water and turns to them. Sam: **"RUN!"**
    Jamie: "GO! GO!" The boy and the creature are never on screen together.
11. **The chase** (about 53–78 s from "RUN!" to the bikes): it comes after them along the drain ("It's coming! IT'S
    COMING!"); is lost round a bend ("Where is it? WHERE IS IT?"); Jamie falls and gets up; it comes **out of a low hole
    in the wall behind them** ("It came out of the wall— it's behind us!"); water bursts from a side pipe; **the old bike
    lies across the way out** ("That's the bike— that's the BIKE—"); at the first bend an old **maintenance gate**: Jamie
    or Sam holds it ("THROUGH! GET THROUGH!"), it is slammed, the creature claws at the bars for about 3 s and bursts
    through; "The bikes! GET TO THE BIKES!"; it stops just inside the mouth and watches. **It never touches anyone.**
12. **The road.** Fast on the bikes. Optional: look back, and when you face front it is half risen at the treeline
    ahead. Farther up, **the boy standing in the road**: "No— no no no—" "Don't stop. DON'T STOP." He steps into the
    trees.
13. **The streetlight.** Sam: **"That wasn't Alex."** Jamie: "…I know." Sam: **"Then what did we follow?"** Jamie does
    not answer. End card.

## Supernatural events

The bell with no ringer; the old bike moving when unobserved; a boy who looks and sounds like Alex but is not; the
creature. Nothing is explained, and the creature is never named.

[Tension and heartbeat](../06_GAMEPLAY_SYSTEMS/TENSION_AND_HEARTBEAT.md) (`tension.js`), adaptive boxless captions

checkpoints, 34 QA jumps, 29 DEV scenes.

## Known problems (accepted limits)

- The creature's locomotion is procedural on a model that only has an idle; simple up close in good light.
- It moves along the drain's centre line; it comes out of the side opening through a black plane.
- Audio is placeholder; nobody has listened.
- SwiftShader only; remaining anatomy, dark-screen legibility and performance need human review on a real GPU.
