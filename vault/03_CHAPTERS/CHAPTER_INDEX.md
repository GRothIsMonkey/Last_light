## How the chapters connect

Chapters hand over without menus: a black screen and a quiet chapter card (Chapters 1→2→3), or an end card with a
**Next chapter** button (Chapters 3→4→5). The DEV selector can start any of 137 scenes. See
[Chapter transitions](../06_GAMEPLAY_SYSTEMS/CHAPTER_TRANSITIONS.md).
