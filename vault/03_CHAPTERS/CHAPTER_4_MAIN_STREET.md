## Setting

Sam's house and garage; Oak Hollow and all of **Summerfield Road**; **Old Mill Road** (about 630 m: older houses, a rise
with the water tower and a steeple, down into town); **downtown Oak Hollow**: Main Street with Mill, Second and Depot
streets, a square, alleys, **Mill Creek**, 27 buildings: Mason Cycle & Sport (closed), the florist, Vic's barber shop,
the Starlite Diner, the Carnegie **library**, a TV shop, the **Lantern Video** store, the laundromat, the **Lyric**
theater. See [Downtown Oak Hollow](../04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/DOWNTOWN_MAIN_STREET.md).

## Starting conditions

Chapter Three's end card has a **Chapter Four →** button. Black, the card, then the next afternoon at Sam's. Officer
Reyes has called Sam's mother again: nothing new.

## Characters

You, Jamie, Sam; **Sam's mom**; **Vic** (barber); **Mr. Dutton** (delivery); **Mrs. Kowalski** (florist); **Mrs. Albright**
("Carol", librarian); townspeople and traffic; the **apparent Alex** (twice); **the creature**; **the Presence** (never
seen). See [Adult characters](../02_CHARACTERS/ADULT_CHARACTERS.md).

## Objectives (summary)

Look in Alex's backpack and camera → find the old bike in his pictures → ride downtown → Mason Cycle & Sport → the
library and microfilm → (dusk, the creature, the lights going out) → the video store → out the back → the Lyric → the
picture → ride home to the old oak. Nothing is timed against you; if you wander, the others call you on.

## Story progression

1. **Sam's mom** in the garage door, tired and careful: "Officer Reyes called again. Nothing new." She found **Alex's
   backpack** in their garage (he left it Saturday). "I want all three of you home before the streetlights come on. I
   mean it."
2. **Alex's camera** (front pocket): a cheap 2009 point-and-shoot. Thirteen ordinary summer pictures (Sam pulling a face,
   Jamie's bunny hop, a dog, Alex's bike, the ice-cream-truck day, the three of them by the oak…), each rendered from this
   world. In the background of **four**, in four places (the field fence by the oak; down Briarwood under a light pole;
   across the creek in the weeds; downtown by a door): **the old bike**. "Wait. That's the bike. The old one." "It's in
   all of them." The last is dated last Wednesday: outside **Mason Cycle & Sport**, Main Street, closed for twelve years.
3. **The ride downtown** along Summerfield and Old Mill Road.
4. **Main Street by day.** An ordinary Tuesday. Vic: closed in '99 "when Roy passed… Library's got all the old
   Couriers." Mason's: faded sign, FOR LEASE, papered windows; through the glass a sun-bleached **MEADOWLARK** poster of a
   green bike: "That's the bike." Mrs. Kowalski: "There's nobody in there, hon. Not since Roy Mason died." "Roy had a lot
   of bikes." "Ask Carol at the desk."
5. **The library and the microfilm** (*The Oak Hollow Courier*), read at a reader that fills the view:
   - **June 14, 1988 — MASON CYCLE MARKS 30 YEARS ON MAIN.** Roy Mason, 61, with the 1958 Meadowlark he built: "First bike
     I ever sold, and I bought it back." It still wears the township's licence sticker **No. 0417**.
   - **August 26, 1991 — SEARCH FOR MISSING BOY ENTERS FIFTH DAY.** **Daniel "Danny" Whitcomb, 12**, last seen riding a
     **green bicycle on Mill Street the evening of August 21**; volunteers searched the creek, the old rail yard and the
     shoreline of the **Pine Ridge reservoir**. ("August twenty-first." "…Twenty years ago.")
   - **September 4, 1991 — 'IT WAS HIM,' NEIGHBORS SAY.** Seen on the Mill Street bridge at dusk almost two weeks later:
     "He didn't answer. He looked at me, and then he walked off toward the creek."
   - **September 11, 1991 — POLICE BLOTTER.** A pale person crouched in the storm culvert under the Mill Street bridge; a
     bicycle bell ringing; nothing found. Streetlights out on Main between Second and Depot, restored by morning.
   - **August 30, 1966 — GIRL, 11, STILL MISSING.** **Ruth Ann Carver**, last seen the evening of **August 23** riding
     downtown to the **Lyric**. Her bicycle has not been found. ("That's today.")
   The afternoon passes a reel at a time until "Boys? I'm sorry. I'm closing up." "Ten to eight." "Eight?"
6. **Dusk.** Across Main, at the gangway beside Mason's: **Alex**. "…Alex?" He looks at them, turns, walks into the gangway
   and is gone. Sam holds Jamie's arm: "Jamie. Don't." Nobody follows. "It wasn't him in the tunnel either." The
   streetlights come on one by one.
7. **The creature, afraid** (Second Street, 15–20 m): it watches ("Stop. Stop." "Don't move."); stalks up the street
   ("It's coming." Sam: **"Don't run. Don't run."**); freezes mid-stride; its head snaps to the TV shop's empty corner,
   whose lights die slowly ("What's it looking at?" "There's nothing there."); it backs away; it **hides**, pressed small
   and shaking into the lit corner of the video store ("It's hiding." "…From what?"). Far up Main, the streetlights begin
   to go out one after another, coming this way. It bolts for the creek.
8. **The TV shop window.** The dark stops just past it; every set comes on: the three of them **from high above, live**
   ("That's us. That's us, right now."). "The video store. Go. GO!"
9. **The Lantern Video** (nobody there; BACK IN 10 MIN — D). The TV over the counter shows Alex from impossible places
   (from forty feet up on Briarwood; in his yard from behind a hedge; at dusk walking past a brown sign that holds:
   **PINE RIDGE RECREATION AREA**). **V** raises Alex's camera; **F** photographs the screen (or Jamie does). Alex in his
   bed at night from the ceiling corner. The phone rings: static, wind, **"…Jamie?"**, dead. The TV shows the three of them
   in the store **now**, from the corner farthest from you; go toward it and it is from another corner ("It moved."). The
   tubes fail from the one over you outward. The front door is the wrong way. The back door.
10. **The escape** — five complete ways from the back door to the Lyric (the laundromat; the narrow way to Depot Street;
    the passage or the gangway to Main; along the creek). **The dark follows the way you actually went**: hesitate and it
    closes, keep moving and it falls back; it never catches you. On every way the creature comes at you **from ahead**,
    a light goes out **behind** you, it stares past you, shrinks, its own end goes dark, and it **bolts past**, close,
    without touching anyone ("It went right past us." "It didn't even look at us."). In the Lyric's upper windows,
    someone stands in each until you look straight at it ("Jamie. The windows.").
11. **The Lyric.** Everything stops: no wind, no insects, nothing going out; the figure under the marquee does not sway
    or breathe. Jamie: "…Alex?" Sam: "Jamie. Don't." Then, as they come nearer:
    - ALEX: **"Did you guys hear that?"** — JAMIE: "…Hear what?" — ALEX: "Never mind."
    - SAM: "That's what he said. On the hill. That's exactly—"
    - ALEX: **"Don't run. Don't run."** — SAM: "…That's what I said."
    - ALEX: **"Alright, I'm this way." "See you tomorrow."**
    The marquee goes out. When the lights come back, he is gone.
12. **The picture.** On Alex's camera: the screen and the brown sign, PINE RIDGE RECREATION AREA. "That's the reservoir.
    Out past the county road." "That's where they looked for Danny Whitcomb. In the paper." "Tomorrow." "…Tomorrow."
13. **Home to the old oak.** "That thing in the tunnel…" "It was scared." "Of what?" A streetlight down the street goes out
    for a moment and comes back. End card.
