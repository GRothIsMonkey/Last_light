# Master timeline

All dates are in-world. Labels: ✅ CONFIRMED CANON · 🟡 IMPLEMENTED BUT UNAPPROVED (Chapter Five) · ❓ UNKNOWN.
"Derived" means the vault computed it from stated facts (for example a weekday), not that the game states it.
The calendar is consistent with the real 2011 calendar (August 21, 2011 was a Sunday).

## Patterns in the dates

- **August 21**: Danny Whitcomb (1991) and Alex (2011) both vanish on this date, both on green bicycles.
- **August 23**: Ruth Ann Carver (1966) vanishes riding to the Lyric; on Aug 23, 2011, the boys meet the false Alex at the
  Lyric ("That's today.").
- **August 24**: two bike tracks in at the camp's service gate in 1991; the boys go to the camp on Aug 24, 2011
  ("Twenty years ago today.").
- Whether these are meaningful is ❓ UNKNOWN (deliberately unexplained).
