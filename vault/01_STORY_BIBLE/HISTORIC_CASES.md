It still wears the township's original licence sticker, **No. 0417**. | 1988-B |

| Wed **September 11, 1991** | POLICE BLOTTER | *Sept. 9, 11:40 p.m.* — a Depot St. resident reports a **pale person crouched in the storm culvert under the Mill Street bridge**, and a **bicycle bell ringing repeatedly**; officers found nothing. *Sept. 10, 2:15 a.m.* — **streetlights out on Main St. between Second and Depot**; restored by morning. | 1991-D |
| Tue **August 30, 1966**, p. 1 | GIRL, 11, STILL MISSING | **Ruth Ann Carver, 11**, not seen since the evening of **August 23**, when she rode her bicycle downtown to the **Lyric Theater**. Her bicycle has not been found. Chief Hollis: "no new information." The case remains open. | 1966-C |

## Camp Whippoorwill, August 1991 (Chapter Five) — 🟡 IMPLEMENTED BUT UNAPPROVED

- The volunteer search for Danny Whitcomb used the camp, closed that week, as its base. Their map is still on the board.
- Register entries cover **August 22–25, 1991**. Read aloud in game: "Police called re: missing boy. Mill Street." ·
  "Light in the cabins." (after it closed) · **8/24, Grid 7 (pump house / service road): "two bike tracks at the service
  gate, fresh. Both lead in. None out."**
- The search grid map: grid seven circled, the pump house; a note: "Bike tracks at gate. 8/24. Not followed up?"
- Lodge photographs run "every summer, sixty-four to ninety. Then it just stops."
