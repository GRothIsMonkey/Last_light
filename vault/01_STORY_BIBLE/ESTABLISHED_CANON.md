## ✅ CONFIRMED CANON (Prologue – Chapter Four)

### The friends

- Four friends: **you** (unnamed, speaker "YOU"), **Jamie**, **Sam**, **Alex**. JSA = Jamie, Sam, Alex (chalk at the lookout). — Ch0
- **Alex** has **no surname** in the game; "AR" can never be his initials. — Ch1 rule
- Alex is **12** ("twelve-year-old male", police radio). — Ch1
- Alex rides a **green cruiser** with a rack, a bell and a red rear reflector; wears a mustard/gold collared shirt and
  olive shorts; owns a red helmet with a white stripe and his number ("He never rides without it. His mom makes him."). — Ch0, Ch1, Ch3
- Jamie lives in a two-story house (red porch chair); Sam in a low ranch with blue garage trim and an old hoop; Alex on
  Briarwood Lane just past the creek. — Ch1
- The player lives in the same neighborhood (Chapter Two's morning starts by your house); the exact street is not yet verified. — Ch2
- The player **moved away in October** 2011 and did not keep in touch; the story is remembered by an adult. — Ch0 narration
- Alex left his backpack (with his camera) in Sam's family garage on Saturday, Aug 20. — Ch4

### The night Alex vanished

- Alex said "Did you guys hear that?" on the hill, then "Never mind." — Ch0
- He left first, turned onto **Briarwood Lane**, rang his bell **twice**, waved, rode out of sight round the curve by the
  creek. — Ch0
- At the turn he **stopped and looked toward the creek** for a few seconds (Sam remembered; the memory confirms). — Ch1, Ch2
- He never got home. His bike was not at home. Last seen on Oak Hollow around eight. — Ch1
- His **broken red reflector** was at the creek; his **bike** lay by the culvert in the easement, reflector broken out of
  the clip. — Ch1, Ch2
- His helmet stayed on his desk that night, and was found deep in the storm drain on Aug 22. — Ch3
- For several nights before (Thu–Sat), he heard a bike bell outside at night; his last recording: "There it is again." — Ch3

### The bell

- A bicycle bell, rung **twice**, is heard where no rider is: beyond the creek fence (Ch1), from deep in the culvert (Ch2),
  in Alex's recording (Ch3), in the storm drain (Ch3), in the 1991 police blotter (Ch4).
- The adults do not take it seriously ("We heard a bell. In there." — "Okay."). — Ch2

### The old bike

- An old sage-green bike with swept bars, a cracked brown saddle, a rusted **bell that does not ring** (only a dry click),
  an "OAK HOLLOW TWP." bicycle licence **No. 0417** (year rubbed off) and scratched initials **AR**. One model is used for
  every appearance (`dist/old-bike.js`). — Ch0, Ch3
- It appears under the oak after the call, only when nobody is looking (Ch0); it is gone that night and absent the next
  morning (Ch1, Ch2); it stands in the storm drain, wet, then is gone, then lies across the way out (Ch3); it is in the
  background of four of Alex's photographs (Ch4).
- It is Roy Mason's **1958 Meadowlark**, the first bike he sold and bought back. — Ch4
- It is never seen moving.

### The lookout

- The chalk JSA; faint chalk **AR** beside it; **AR** carved in the oak at a kid's height; a chalk drawing of four riders
  that gains a **fifth** only when unobserved. — Ch0
- "I thought I remembered everyone." — Ch0

### Places

- Oak Hollow: Oak Hollow Drive, Briarwood Lane, Summerfield Road, the creek strip, the drainage easement and culvert, the
  stormwater access road from the end of Briarwood, the outfall and the 286 m storm drain, Old Mill Road, downtown (Main,
  Mill, Second, Depot streets; Mill Creek). — Ch0–4. See [Locations](../04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/LOCATION_INDEX.md).
- The creek behind Alex's house runs to the culvert and on to the storm drain ("Your creek ends up down there."). — Ch3
- Mason Cycle & Sport closed in 1999 when Roy Mason died. — Ch4
- *The Oak Hollow Courier* is on microfilm at the Carnegie library. — Ch4
- Pine Ridge Recreation Area is "the reservoir. Out past the county road", where Danny Whitcomb was searched for. — Ch4

### The supernatural

- A figure that **looks and sounds like Alex** but is not him: in the drain (Ch3), at the gangway and under the Lyric
  marquee (Ch4), in the road (Ch3). It waits to be seen; it walks away; it repeats words that were said (including
  where it was not present). — Ch3, Ch4
- **The creature**: pale, thin, long-armed, on all fours, a wide red grin of teeth, hollow eyes. Revealed at the culvert
  where the false Alex went in; chases them through the drain; **never touches anyone**; at dusk downtown it is
  **afraid**, hides, and flees the coming dark; it runs past the boys without looking at them. — Ch3, Ch4
- The false Alex and the creature are **never on screen together**. — Ch3
- **The darkness that takes the lights** (dev name: the Presence): streetlights going out in a line toward them; TVs showing
  them live from impossible angles; a phone call ("…Jamie?"); figures in the Lyric's windows that vanish when looked at
  directly; total stillness under the marquee. Never shown, never named. — Ch4
- Historic pattern: Ruth Ann Carver (1966) and Danny Whitcomb (1991) vanished on bikes in late August; 1991 also had a pale
  figure in a culvert, a bell, and streetlights out on Main. — Ch4

### Things that are not true

- Nothing implies injury: no blood, no body has been found. — Ch2 onward
- No school scenes; school has not started. — Ch2
- No lab, no powers. — Ch1 rule

## 🟡 Working baseline: Chapter Five (IMPLEMENTED BUT UNAPPROVED)

- On Aug 24 the boys ride to Pine Ridge Park (about four miles) and match Alex's picture at the old gravel lot.
- Backstory told there: the boys came to Pine Ridge "five" times the summer before; Alex fished off the dock for three
  hours and caught a shoe he named **Gary**, which he keeps on the trophy shelf in his garage.
- Camp Whippoorwill (est. 1961, closed) was the base of the 1991 search; the register's 8/24/1991 entry: two bike tracks
  in at the service gate, none out; grid seven, the pump house; "not followed up".
- Someone has recently taken cans from the pantry.
- **Alex's gold shirt**, still wet, hung in Owl cabin; **JSA** freshly carved with four marks (Sun–Wed) and an arrow
  toward the pump house. Sam keeps the shirt in his bag.
- Lights that copy the boys; voices of Alex and of Sam's mother; the camp pulls them apart; **Jamie's double** walks out
  of Owl toward them; every light comes on, then goes out.
- The creature fears the carried lights.
- A photograph of the three of them at the campfire, taken from the trees, is on Alex's camera, which never left the
  player's pocket.
- All three boys get out. "Tomorrow we tell somebody." One light remains in Owl.
