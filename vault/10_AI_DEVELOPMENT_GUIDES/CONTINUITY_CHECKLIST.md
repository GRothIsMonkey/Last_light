# Continuity checklist

Run this on any new dialogue, scene, clue or chapter. Copy it into your handoff with the boxes ticked.

## Time and place

- [ ] The in-world date and time follow the [Master timeline](../01_STORY_BIBLE/MASTER_TIMELINE.md) (Sun Aug 21 → Wed
      Aug 24, 2011 so far; Chapter Five ends ~8:25 PM at the Pine Ridge lot).
- [ ] Weekdays are right (Aug 21, 2011 = Sunday).
- [ ] Places match [Locations](../04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/LOCATION_INDEX.md) (street names, which side, what is enterable).
- [ ] Light and weather fit the time of day.

## People

- [ ] Jamie, Sam and the player sound like themselves ([Jamie](../02_CHARACTERS/JAMIE.md), [Sam](../02_CHARACTERS/SAM.md),
      [Player](../02_CHARACTERS/PLAYER.md)).
- [ ] Their looks, clothes and bikes match `CAST` ([Character design](../07_VISUAL_AND_AUDIO/CHARACTER_DESIGN.md)).
- [ ] Alex has **no surname**; "AR" is not Alex.
- [ ] What each character knows matches what they have seen (e.g. adults have not seen anything supernatural).
- [ ] Parents' situations are consistent (Sam's mom at work till six on Aug 24; she thinks they are at Jamie's).

## Objects and clues

- [ ] Who carries what: the player has Alex's camera; Sam has the shirt, the county map, batteries, water; Jamie has his
      bag and the sock (end of Chapter Five).
- [ ] The old bike matches its one build (sage Meadowlark, No. 0417, AR, a bell that only clicks); it is never seen moving.
- [ ] New clues are added to the [clue tracker](../01_STORY_BIBLE/MYSTERY_AND_CLUE_TRACKER.md).

## Quotes

- [ ] Any line that quotes an earlier chapter quotes it **exactly** as the accepted code has it. (Known failure: "It said
      'come find him.'" — OI-01.) Check `dist/chapterN.js`, not the README.
- [ ] The marquee figure's words in Chapter Four: "Did you guys hear that?" / "Never mind." / "Don't run. Don't run." /
      "Alright, I'm this way." / "See you tomorrow."

## The supernatural

- [ ] The Presence and the creature stay **unnamed and unexplained**.
- [ ] The creature and the false Alex are **never on screen together**.
- [ ] The creature's established behavior (never touched anyone; afraid of the Presence) is respected — or the change is a
      recorded human decision.
- [ ] No gore, symbols or glitch effects.
- [ ] Every threat is perceivable and caused ([Horror design bible](../05_HORROR_DESIGN/HORROR_DESIGN_BIBLE.md)).

## Status

- [ ] Every new fact carries a label (✅ / 🟡 / 💡 / ❓ / ⛔).
- [ ] Nothing 💡 was promoted to canon without a human decision.
- [ ] Dependencies on Chapter Five (🟡) are listed.
- [ ] [Unanswered questions](../01_STORY_BIBLE/UNANSWERED_QUESTIONS.md) were not answered by accident.
